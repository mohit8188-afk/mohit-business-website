How we work

Understand first. Propose second.

No assumptions or one-size-fits-all packages. We discuss your needs before deciding what to build.

STEP 01

Discovery

Discuss your business, current process and desired outcomes.

STEP 02

Requirements

Document priorities, users, existing tools and constraints.

STEP 03

Proposal

Agree on a suitable approach, scope, timeline and quotation.

STEP 04

Implementation

Begin only after the scope and commercial terms are approved.

Practical by design: I won't recommend a tool simply because it is new or popular. Any proposed solution should fit the business, be feasible to support, and have clear costs and responsibilities.
