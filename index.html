Frequently asked questions

A few things worth knowing.

Business improvement should begin with clarity, not complexity.

What can we discuss in the free call?

Your current operations, repetitive tasks, order or customer follow-ups, reporting challenges, existing software and the outcomes you want to improve. The first conversation is designed to identify whether there's a useful opportunity to explore.

Do I need to know which software or AI tool I want?

No. We start with the business problem. Sometimes a simpler process or better use of an existing system is more valuable than introducing new software or AI.

Will you replace our existing systems?

Not automatically. We first consider whether your current tools can be improved or connected. A new system is suggested only where it is justified.

How do you determine project costs?

Every business is different. After understanding the requirements, we propose a defined scope, implementation approach and commercial terms tailored to the work involved.

What happens after the discovery call?

If there is a potential fit, we discuss the next step—such as a detailed process audit—and agree on its scope before any further work begins.
