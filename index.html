Frequently asked questions

Clarity before commitment.

We prefer a useful conversation to selling a package that may not fit.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We initially focus on owner-managed manufacturers, distributors, B2B suppliers and other growing businesses seeking practical operational or digital improvements. We evaluate each enquiry individually.

Do you offer fixed packages?

No. The work depends on your processes and priorities. After an introductory discussion and feasibility review, we propose options with a defined scope and quotation.

Is the first discussion free?

Yes, the initial conversation to understand your needs is free. Detailed audits, implementation and ongoing service are priced separately if you choose to proceed.

Can a project be one-time or monthly?

Either. Depending on the need, we may suggest a one-time improvement, a setup project with optional support, or an ongoing service. Commercial terms are confirmed in writing.

Will you need to replace our current software?

Not necessarily. We first look for ways to use existing tools and processes better. We recommend new systems only where feasibility and business value justify them.

What happens after we discuss the problem?

If it looks feasible, we clarify the requirements, research suitable approaches and propose next steps. We never start paid work without an agreed scope, responsibilities and approval.
