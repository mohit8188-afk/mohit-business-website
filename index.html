Frequently asked questions

Know what you're paying for.

We provide evidence-backed prospect research, not a guarantee of interested buyers or sales.

What is a billable lead?

A unique researched company matching the agreed customer profile, required information fields and documented evidence standard. It is not automatically an interested buyer, a booked meeting or a completed sale.

How much does each lead cost?

We agree a price per accepted researched lead before work begins. Rates vary by geography, industry, complexity, required information and verification depth. No monthly retainer is required for this research service.

Does each record include a verified email or phone number?

Not necessarily. We provide public business contact routes where available. Verified personal inboxes, direct-dial numbers and otherwise unavailable contacts are not included unless separately agreed and feasible.

Will you send emails or arrange sales meetings?

No. This service is research and company qualification. Your sales team handles outreach, meetings, negotiations and closing.

What if a record is duplicate or off-target?

We review records against the agreed lead definition and sources. The acceptance, dispute and correction process is agreed before work starts.

Who is this for?

Our initial focus is manufacturers, industrial suppliers, wholesalers, distributors and B2B professional-service businesses looking for relevant companies to approach.
