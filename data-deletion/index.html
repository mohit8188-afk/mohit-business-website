Project01 WACRM

Data Deletion Instructions

Use these instructions to request deletion of personal information handled by Project01 WACRM or associated business services operated by Mohit Sharma – Business Improvement & Digital Services.

Submit a deletion request

  1. Email mohit@mohitsharma.click with the subject “WACRM data deletion request”.
  2. Provide the phone number or email associated with the information and identify the relevant business/account or conversation, if known. Specify what you want deleted.
  3. We will verify the requester's identity or authority as needed and respond with the outcome or any additional information required. Never send passwords, access tokens or one-time codes.

What happens next

For information under our control, we will assess the request, remove eligible records from active systems, and explain any applicable exceptions. This may include contact records, conversation data and related account information. Records required for legal obligations, security or dispute resolution may need to be retained; encrypted backup copies may persist after active records are deleted until they can be securely disposed of. We account for outstanding deletion requests before restoring information to active use. We will communicate the expected handling time after reviewing the request, subject to applicable legal and platform deadlines.

WhatsApp and Meta access

Business account administrators can revoke application access using the relevant Meta business settings. Disconnecting the app does not by itself delete previously stored CRM information; submit a deletion request if needed. Information stored by Meta or another business is subject to that organization's own deletion procedures and policies. We cannot delete information outside our control.

Questions and opt-out

Contact mohit@mohitsharma.click for deletion questions or to opt out of business marketing messages. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
