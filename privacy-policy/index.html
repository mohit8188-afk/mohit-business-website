Project01 WACRM

Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Mohit Sharma – Business Improvement & Digital Services (Ahmedabad, India, “we”) handles information in connection with this business website and Project01 WACRM, our WhatsApp customer relationship management application. Contact us at mohit@mohitsharma.click.

Information we handle

  • Business enquiry and contact details you choose to send, including name, company, phone number, email and enquiry content.
  • For authorized CRM use: WhatsApp profile and contact information, messages and attachments where supported, message identifiers and delivery status, conversation history, notes, and staff account and access information.
  • Authorized Meta account identifiers, permissions and integration credentials needed to connect and operate the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Technical information associated with service delivery, troubleshooting, hosting and security.

Our website enquiry form prepares an email draft; it does not automatically transmit or store your answers in a website lead database. Information is received when you choose to send the email or communicate through another channel.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, manage authorized customer conversations and follow-ups, deliver agreed services, administer access, maintain the integration, troubleshoot problems, and meet applicable obligations. We use WhatsApp messaging for permitted business communications and respect opt-out requests.

Meta and AI processing

WACRM uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to exchange messages through authorized business accounts. When AI assistance is enabled, relevant conversation content and business instructions may be processed by OpenAI to generate or help staff prepare replies. Automated responses may be inaccurate; staff should review important responses. We do not authorize use of Meta Platform Data to train generalized AI models. AI use remains subject to applicable Meta terms and the configured service terms.

Service providers and sharing

Providers involved in service delivery may include Meta for WhatsApp, OpenAI for configured AI functionality, hosting and infrastructure providers for the website and CRM, and Google for email and appointment booking. Their services may involve processing in other countries. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or to protect legitimate security interests. We do not sell personal information.

Where a business uses its own independently managed CRM and WhatsApp account, it is responsible for its own customer communications and applicable notices; our access and processing depend on the agreed service scope.

Security and retention

We use restricted account access and separated CRM infrastructure, and take reasonable steps to protect integration credentials and stored information. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for the time reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry or service, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Backup copies may remain until their applicable retention cycle expires.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or opt out of marketing communications. We may need to verify your identity or authority. For deletion instructions, see Data Deletion. For information controlled by another business, contact that business as well.

External services and updates

Links to third-party services such as WhatsApp and Google Calendar are subject to their own policies. We may update this policy as our services change. The current policy will be available on this page.
